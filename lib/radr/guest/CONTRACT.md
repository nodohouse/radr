# Guest Value

## Purpose

Help hospitality teams understand **who is coming**, **returning revenue**, and **service-relevant context** — not build a CRM or surveillance tool.

## Rules

- Explainable tiers only: RETURNING · FREQUENT · HIGH-VALUE · LAPSED · FIRST-TIME · GROUP ORGANIZER
- Never black-box “VIP scores”
- No sensitive inferred attributes
- Identity merge requires confidence; never merge on similar names alone
- Role-based access: finance/CFO aggregates; GM/host get service context
- Surface on Control Center **only when material**

## Returning revenue

Revenue from guests with prior attributable visits. Always labeled expected / historical / projected — never guaranteed.
