# Registration → demo → plan selection

The intended live journey is: register a complete account, verify email ownership, explore a fictional hotel or restaurant, compare plans, then activate the chosen scope. The user confirmed that no database or email provider is available yet. The current release is therefore an explicitly labeled registration and conversion preview, not a real identity, email-verification or payment service.

The public chooser preserves hotel/restaurant selection into registration. The form requires name, company, email, role, location count, hospitality type, a sample password, matching confirmation and terms/privacy acknowledgement. Shared validation blocks missing values, obvious placeholders, malformed text, repeated junk, known example/disposable email domains and common passwords. International names, apostrophes, accents and legitimate free-email accounts remain supported. Heuristics cannot establish a real person's identity or prove company legitimacy. Live email verification remains required before actual account access is activated.

Registration details are sent to `/api/demo/access` only for validation and then discarded. Passwords and confirmation never leave the browser. The API rejects credentials, unknown keys, incomplete details, honeypots and unexpected destinations; it checks origin, size and request frequency. It stores no contacts, creates no user and sends no email. A signed eight-hour HTTP-only grant opens fictional DEMO screens only. Its new `registration-preview:v2` scope invalidates grants issued by the old, vertical-only guest flow. Middleware and product layout still verify it; LIVE and SANDBOX never accept it as a real account session. Real workspace/API authorization remains separate.

The demo opens without a pricing banner or plan prompt. A small non-modal invitation can appear after four minutes of visible, focused demo use with interaction, or after two minutes spent exploring at least three sections. It waits for an eight-second quiet moment and defers during dialogs, text entry, open navigation drawers and dragging. It never redirects or takes keyboard focus. Keep exploring, the close button or Escape dismiss it; showing it once caps it for the tab session, including route changes and reloads when session storage is available. Returning to the demo after viewing plans does not repeat it. Only a deliberate Explore plans click carries the hotel/restaurant context into pricing. All three plan choices preserve their ID into setup. Demo-origin pricing goes directly to the setup preview; public pricing goes through required registration first. Setup visibly states that no account, payment or subscription has started. Plan selection is an expression of interest, not a billing entitlement. It never calls the default trial-subscription factory or activates writes.

The animated entry showcase remains continuous, with reduced-motion support and no playback controls. Required-field errors are associated with their inputs. Policy links open in another tab so they do not discard form work.

Before real activation: provision persistent account storage, persist registration profiles, enable an actual verification email service, enforce verified identity and completed profile on server routes, and connect approved commercial activation/checkout. Do not turn the limited preview grant into real workspace authentication or present its completion as account creation.

## Standard packages and preview boundaries

The founder has no employees or customer-success team. The pricing model must therefore be a repeatable product offering: Core, Expand and Portfolio, with standard guided setup and documentation. No package includes a dedicated CSM, bespoke implementation, managed data cleanup, custom connector development, migration labor, scheduled training or a response-time SLA. Operator review means the customer's team, not a RADR employee.

The user explicitly requested that prices remain pending. Publish no proposed numeric rates, annual discount, individually tailored quote or numeric usage allowance. The packages describe one location, up to five and up to twenty respectively. Standard guided setup is included in the proposed packages, rather than a paid implementation engagement. Monthly prices, usage limits, billing, cancellation and renewal terms must be finalized and published before paid activation. No checkout or subscription is activated by this preview.

`start`, `expand` and `enterprise` remain stable public URL IDs. Their display names are Core, Expand and Portfolio. Older contact IDs (`pilot`, `core`, `control`) retain compatibility with those display names. Plan selection previews setup; it does not start a subscription, activate usage billing or change existing entitlement code. Public choices retain registration first; demo-origin choices retain the selected world into setup. The existing quiet, optional plan invitation stays delayed; fresh demo visitors see no immediate upsell.

Before paid activation, implement and verify server-side plan entitlement and usage accounting, upgrade/limit handling, payment lifecycle and cancellation. Structured-record allowances do not bound model/API cost, media processing or refresh work; budget those separately before activating any paid feature. No numeric allowance or arbitrary provider connection is promised by the package preview. Verify supported templates and source requirements instead of promising automated migration of any file.

`docs/product/pricing-model.json` records the proposed founder-operated model and its cost-floor checks. Founder time is a cost even without payroll. Final rates require cost and willingness-to-pay validation; do not label pending prices profit-maximizing, verified margins or proven customer ROI.
