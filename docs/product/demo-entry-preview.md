# Registration → demo → plan selection

The intended live journey is: register a complete account, verify email ownership, explore a fictional hotel or restaurant, compare plans, then activate the chosen scope. The user confirmed that no database or email provider is available yet. The current release is therefore an explicitly labeled registration and conversion preview, not a real identity, email-verification or payment service.

The public chooser preserves hotel/restaurant selection into registration. The form requires name, company, email, role, location count, hospitality type, a sample password, matching confirmation and terms/privacy acknowledgement. Shared validation blocks missing values, obvious placeholders, malformed text, repeated junk, known example/disposable email domains and common passwords. International names, apostrophes, accents and legitimate free-email accounts remain supported. Heuristics cannot establish a real person's identity or prove company legitimacy. Live email verification remains required before actual account access is activated.

Registration details are sent to `/api/demo/access` only for validation and then discarded. Passwords and confirmation never leave the browser. The API rejects credentials, unknown keys, incomplete details, honeypots and unexpected destinations; it checks origin, size and request frequency. It stores no contacts, creates no user and sends no email. A signed eight-hour HTTP-only grant opens fictional DEMO screens only. Its new `registration-preview:v2` scope invalidates grants issued by the old, vertical-only guest flow. Middleware and product layout still verify it; LIVE and SANDBOX never accept it as a real account session. Real workspace/API authorization remains separate.

The demo opens without a pricing banner or plan prompt. A small non-modal invitation can appear after four minutes of visible, focused demo use with interaction, or after two minutes spent exploring at least three sections. It waits for an eight-second quiet moment and defers during dialogs, text entry, open navigation drawers and dragging. It never redirects or takes keyboard focus. Keep exploring, the close button or Escape dismiss it; showing it once caps it for the tab session, including route changes and reloads when session storage is available. Returning to the demo after viewing plans does not repeat it. Only a deliberate Explore plans click carries the hotel/restaurant context into pricing. All three plan choices preserve their ID into setup. Demo-origin pricing goes directly to the setup preview; public pricing goes through required registration first. Setup visibly states that no account, payment or subscription has started. Plan selection is an expression of interest, not a billing entitlement. It never calls the default trial-subscription factory or activates writes.

The animated entry showcase remains continuous, with reduced-motion support and no playback controls. Required-field errors are associated with their inputs. Policy links open in another tab so they do not discard form work.

Before real activation: provision persistent account storage, persist registration profiles, enable an actual verification email service, enforce verified identity and completed profile on server routes, and connect approved commercial activation/checkout. Do not turn the limited preview grant into real workspace authentication or present its completion as account creation.

## Shared presentation contract

The product uses the RADR-only browser-tab title and a shared working type scale. Shared metadata and evidence labels use a 12px base; the decorative marketing tablet retains its own display scale. Live uses a compact context strip so the spatial operation remains accessible without a stack of repeated summary panels. Financial impact captions name the economic status, location and horizon. Public D-1911 confidence is qualitative, matching the product record rather than presenting an uncalibrated percentage.

Hotel Ask answers are grounded in the current selected Decision records and, for Mercer House, its existing room/stay snapshot. Another property does not inherit Mercer guest needs. Answers are read-only and link to the evidence or room workflow. Hotel Connections and Data Health expose provider-specific quality criteria, identifiers, review steps and current Decision evidence without inventing connected-source scores. Settings audit examples follow the selected world and show that they are illustrative review history.

Editorial pages whose current copy is English-only resolve to their English URL. Canonicals, sitemap entries and language alternates follow that publication policy; translated account-entry routes remain available. A locale-shaped URL does not certify a translation. Real service activation, external data validation and company legal identity remain separate launch work.

Regression coverage includes `tests/e2e/scoped-workspace.spec.ts` for hotel Ask, audit filters and provider-specific evidence review. Live camera, layout dragging, generated-building validation and no-WebGL fallback remain covered by their existing browser suites. The building shows its elevation while the 3D renderer initializes, then hands over to the interactive scene.

## Marketing preview links

Platform navigation uses `/product?surface=<id>#surfaces`. The passive gallery opens on the requested surface, including after a client navigation between two surface links. Unknown values fall back to the Control Center. It then continues its visible-page film sequence, with a static selection under reduced motion. The only action inside the gallery is the demo link; no product controls, canvases or workspace sessions are mounted on the marketing page. Solutions links similarly carry their selected leak and the `value-leaks` anchor.

Marketing frames are captures of the current fictional hotel and restaurant product. They include the current Data Health and Connections workbenches. `PLATFORM_GALLERY_REVISION` changes with refreshed assets so image optimization caches cannot serve the older frames. The source files under `public/platform-showcase/` remain raster previews; they never imply a connected customer system. The marketing navigation regression covers every public surface and a client transition between two feature links.

## Commercial scope and preview boundaries

The public model is Core, Expand and Enterprise, all paid annual scopes. The fictional demo is free after the existing registration preview; it is not a free customer-data trial. A recurring quote has a platform minimum, named locations and explicit processing/support allowances. One-time onboarding, data cleanup, historical backfills and custom integration work are separately scoped and priced. Growth requires an approved scope and price change. Evidence review and operator responsibility are common to all scopes; neither is a premium-only trust feature.

`start`, `expand` and `enterprise` remain stable public plan IDs. `start` displays as Core. Older contact IDs (`pilot`, `core`, `control`) remain compatibility keys for Core, Expand and Enterprise respectively. No plan choice starts a subscription or changes existing entitlement code. Public choices continue through the registration preview; demo-origin choices preserve the selected world into the setup preview. There is no new checkout, automatic overage charge or success fee.

Before issuing real quotes, validate recurring delivery costs, launch labor, acquisition costs and willingness to pay. `docs/product/pricing-model.json` records the pricing rationale, cost-floor formulas and non-binding sensitivity examples. These are internal assumptions, not public prices or measured RADR margins. Do not describe customer revenue as RADR gross profit or use expected savings to invoice a success fee.
