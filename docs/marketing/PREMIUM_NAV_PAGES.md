## Scope

The five main navigation destinations share `app/marketing-premium-pages.css`, scoped to `.radr.rm-premium`: Platform (`/product`), Solutions, Value Calculator, Pricing and Company. It does not restyle the homepage tablet or authenticated application. The existing public shell owns the approved frozen logo, navigation, footer and Manrope typeface. Sapphire-to-ocean backgrounds frame the product and commercial story. Jade marks active choices and primary actions. Neutral glass cards keep body text readable. Entrance transitions respect reduced motion. Product previews remain explicitly synthetic.

Quotes and actual delivery require agreement.

The previous `pricing/config.ts` and translated feature matrix remain for legacy components; they do not drive the current pricing page. Do not reintroduce their inconsistent Start / Operating Intelligence / Expand package names on this surface.
