# Brutal 15 — Margin & Recovery discovery

Questions first. Demo only on **their** real variance. Never lead with Coke.

## Setup (2 min)

1. Who owns AP / purchasing exceptions today?
2. How many sites? (~8–30 FSR / FC is D0 ICP)
3. Stack: AP tool · contract store · POS or menu/recipe source?
4. What’s the last credit or price variance that sat “on screen” unpaid?

## Wound hunt (8 min)

5. Credit issued but never applied — last 90 days, how many / €?
6. Two sites, same supplier, different unit price — seen it?
7. Short-ship / weight / UOM traps — who catches them?
8. Permanent “temporary” surcharge still flowing?
9. Unclaimed rebate or deposit/crate floats?
10. Duplicate invoice or VAT mishandled on credits?

## Decision test (3 min)

11. If we showed **one** Margin Response card (Futures + REC + € Expected + Trace), who acts?
12. Would Finance trust Verified € only with invoice → evidence → CM applied + doc_ref?
13. What would kill the pilot: second GL fear · rip-replace · fake dashboards?

## Close (2 min)

14. 14-day margin recovery pilot — 2–3 sites · 2–3 feeds · clean exit — yes/no?
15. Which wound do we open first on day one? (**Their** case — not our catalog.)

## Demo rule

Only after Q4–Q10 land: open `?seed=recover` (credit-not-applied) **or** `?seed=margin-response` (two-site gap) — whichever matches their wound.  
One card → Trace → stop. No labor tour. No Wait-12. No hotel. No autopilot sprinkle.
